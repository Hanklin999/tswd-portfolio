About me

Hi, I’m Chih-Han Lin, and I go by Hank. I’m pursuing a Master of Information Systems Management with a concentration in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics (MISM-BIDA) at Carnegie Mellon University’s Heinz College, graduating in 2027.

My undergraduate background is in Risk Management and Insurance at National Chengchi University in Taiwan. My professional experience includes work at Shopee and responsibilities in financial planning and analysis (FP&A) and operations analytics. These experiences shaped my interest in using data to understand business performance and support practical decisions.

At CMU, I’m building on that business foundation by strengthening my technical skills in programming, data analysis, and machine learning. I’m interested in connecting business questions with rigorous analysis—and making the results understandable to the people who need to act on them.

What I hope to learn

In my previous work, analyzing a problem and explaining the findings were closely connected. Through this course, I want to become more deliberate about how I turn analysis into a clear, useful story.

Specifically, I hope to:

  • Choose charts that fit the question, data, and intended audience.
  • Explain complex findings without losing important context or uncertainty.
  • Use visual hierarchy, annotations, and narrative structure to guide readers.
  • Identify misleading visualizations and improve them through critique and revision.
  • Create interactive visualizations that help people explore the evidence.

After graduation, I hope to pursue work that combines my business and operations experience with stronger technical capabilities in analytics and machine learning. I want to use data not only to describe what happened, but also to help people decide what to do next.
